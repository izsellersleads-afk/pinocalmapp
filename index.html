How it works

In the moment,
you'll know what to do.

1

Your child is upset

Big feeling arrives. You reach for your Pino Calm card instead of reacting.

2

Get low, speak softly

Use the parent script on the card. Fewer words, slower voice, stay close.

3

Do the activity together

Balloon Breaths, Feeling Detective, Safe Hands Reset — one simple tool.

4

Reconnect after

Connection comes before correction. Hug first. Talk gently. Try again.
